Zabimaru: NY Opening Range, Octant Grid, Triplet Card & Session Opens

DESCRIPTION (paste everything below the line into the Description field)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

[b]Zabimaru[/b] draws the New York opening range (09:30–10:00 ET) as the morning's dealing range, live as it forms.
• While it forms, an [b]octant grid[/b] (quarters emphasised) follows price as the range delivers.
• Once set, the OR's high, low, grid and extensions run right in price.
• The [b]session opens[/b] (18:00, midnight and a custom time) are built in: the Hihiō section.
• A card reads the OR against ATR10 and whether volatility is rising or falling.
• It gives the odds of each side and of a 1× extension, and sets NQ, ES and RTY side by side (the triplet).
• The odds come from about [b]6,400 opening ranges[/b] on NQ, ES and RTY (2018–26).
[i]A map of the morning's range and how ranges like it usually behave. It does not call direction.[/i]


[b]█ WHAT IT DRAWS ON PRICE[/b]

• [b]The opening range[/b] (09:30–10:00) as a shaded box that grows while it forms.
• [b]A size marker[/b] left of the box: points, % of the open, and × ATR10.
• [b]The octant grid[/b], from 09:30:
  – the OR high and low, EQ (50%), quarters (25 / 75%) and eighths;
  – [b]it follows price while the range forms:[/b] every level is re-drawn at its share of the high–low so far, so the whole grid moves as price delivers;
  – the quarters are emphasised by default, and every tier has its own colour, width and style;
  – at 10:00 it runs on with the OR lines, or stops at 10:00 if set to [i]While forming[/i]. The high and low always run on.
• The OR lines run to 11:00, 12:00, 16:00 or 16:59.
• [b]Extensions[/b] in OR sizes beyond the high and the low: ±0.5× (dotted), ±1× (dashed) and ±2× (solid).
• [b]The first break[/b] after 10:00, labelled at its candle (e.g. [i]▲ 10:05 · ES ✓ · RTY ✓[/i]). Each sister gets ✓ if it broke the same side within 5 minutes, or ✗ if it did not.
• The last N sessions stay on the chart (default 3).


[b]█ SESSION OPENS (HIHIŌ)[/b]

• The [b]18:00 open[/b] (the trading day's open), the [b]midnight open[/b] (00:00 ET, ICT's true day open), and an optional [b]custom open[/b] (08:30 by default).
• Each starts at its time and runs:
  – to 16:59 of the session it belongs to (the 18:00 open belongs to the next day's session);
  – across N sessions;
  – or with price.
• Each fades once RTH trades through it, and its tooltip gives the time.
• They are read from 15-minute bars of the extended session, so the prices are the same on ETH and RTH charts.
• [b]Tested:[/b] RTH traded back to the midnight open on 68% of days, against 66% for a level the same distance on the other side of the 09:30 open. For the 18:00 open it was 64% against 61%. Neither difference held in both periods on its own. [i]Reference lines, not magnets.[/i]


[b]█ CARD[/b]

Four columns: the row, a bar, the value, the result. A row that has happened turns into a green [b]✓[/b] cell with the time.
• [b]OR:[/b] points and %, and × ATR10 with its percentile against the symbol's own ORs (the bar). While it forms, it shows the range so far.
• [b]ATR10:[/b] the mean daily true range of the last 10 sessions, in points and %. The bar shows ATR5 against ATR10. [i]▲ rising[/i] when the last 5 sessions run above ATR10, [i]▼ falling[/i] when below.
• [b]FIRST:[/b] from 10:00, the odds that the high or the low breaks first. Once one breaks, the side and the time.
• [b]HIGH[/b] and [b]LOW:[/b] the odds that each trades by 16:14, from 10:00. Once one side breaks, the other row shows the odds of [i]both[/i] sides being taken.
• [b]1× EXT:[/b] one OR size beyond the broken side, and the odds of reaching it by 16:14 from the break.
• [b]TRIPLET:[/b] NQ, ES and RTY side by side: OR %, × ATR10, its percentile (bar), and each one's breaks. The chart's own symbol is marked ›.
• [b]SISTERS:[/b] whether the other two broke the same side within 5 minutes of the chart's break.
• [b]OPENS:[/b] the latest 18:00 and midnight opens, and ✓ once RTH has traded through each.
• [b]ORG:[/b] the opening range gap (the prior RTH close to the 09:30 open), and whether its CE or fill traded. Benihime maps every RTH gap in full.
• The header pill: [i]PRE, BUILDING, SET, ▲ BREAK, ▼ BREAK, BOTH[/i].

[b]Mobile[/b] shows one narrow card: OR, FIRST, HI/LO, the triplet in one line, and VOL. Every row keeps its full explanation in the tooltip.


[b]█ ALERTS[/b]

Create one alert with [i]Any alert() function call[/i]. Each fires once, on the bar it happens, with the numbers in the message:
• the OR set at 10:00 (range, × ATR10, percentile, first-break odds);
• the first break (time, the 1× level and its odds, the odds of the other side);
• 1× extension reached;
• the other side taken.


[b]█ WHAT THE TESTS SHOWED[/b] (NQ, ES and RTY 1-minute, 2018–26)

Each result below held pooled (p < 0.05, errors clustered by date), in 2018–24 and in 2025–26, and on each of NQ, ES and RTY.

[b]The OR against ATR10 does most of the work.[/b]
• A typical OR is 0.32× ATR10 on NQ, 0.27× on ES and 0.37× on RTY, steady in every year since 2018.
• As % of the open (medians): NQ 0.60%, ES 0.37%, RTY 0.74%.
• By OR size against ATR10 (quartiles: under 0.25×, 0.25–0.32×, 0.32–0.43×, over 0.43×):
  – 1× reached beyond the first broken side: 57%, 47%, 41%, 29%;
  – both sides taken: 54%, 44%, 35%, 26%.

[b]Volatility rising or falling[/b] (ATR5 against ATR10):
• When rising, the OR is larger against ATR10, the RTH range is larger, and 1× extensions and two-sided days come more often.
• [i]ATR10 against its level 5 days earlier showed no such effect, so it is not used.[/i]

[b]The first break[/b]
• Where the 10:00 close sits in the OR decides which side usually breaks first. From the top quarter the high went first 90% of the time; from the bottom quarter, 12%.
• [i]It is not a direction call:[/i] the close ended beyond the first broken side 51% of the time.
• A later break goes less far, and the other side is taken less often.

[b]The triplet (sister edge)[/b]
• When the sisters broke the same side within 5 minutes, the chart's 1× extension came about 3 points more often per sister. The close ended beyond the broken side about 2 points more often.
• [i]Small, but it held.[/i] A lone break did not fail more often.
• An OR that is large against its sisters' (by percentile) extended slightly less.

[b]What did not help[/b]
• the sisters' place in their own ORs at 10:00 (for which side breaks first);
• the 10:00 location as a call for the rest of the day (it did not hold in 2025–26 on its own).

The live odds are logistic models. Fitted on 2018–24 and scored on 2025–26, they beat the base rates by 5–40% (Brier). The biggest gain is for which side breaks first.


[b]█ SETTINGS[/b]

[b]View[/b] sits at the top, the same as in my other indicators:
• card theme;
• text size;
• chart text colour;
• card on/off and position;
• layout: Desktop or Mobile;
• sessions on the chart.

[b]Opening range:[/b] colour, shading and its colour, how far the lines run, size marker, break labels.

[b]Octant grid:[/b] show it forming and after 10:00, only while forming, or off; labels (edges, quarters and EQ; all; off). Each tier has its own colour, width (1–4) and style:
• high / low;
• EQ;
• quarters;
• eighths.

[b]Extensions:[/b] 0.5×, 1× and 2×, each on or off with its own colour, width and style.

[b]Session opens (Hihiō):[/b]
• 18:00, 00:00 and custom (02:00 to 13:30), each with its own colour, width and style;
• how far each line runs (trading day, sessions, with price);
• how many to keep;
• labels (time, time and price, off);
• dim once traded through.

[b]Triplet:[/b] on or off. Auto picks NQ1!, ES1! and RTY1!, or MNQ1!, MES1! and M2K1! on a micro chart. Custom takes any three symbols.

[b]Alerts:[/b] each one on or off.


[b]█ HOW TO USE[/b]

• Built for CME index futures: [b]NQ / MNQ, ES / MES, RTY / M2K[/b].
• Use a chart of 30 minutes or less; 1 minute is recommended. Each break time is read to the chart's bar.
• Use a continuous, back-adjusted chart ([i]B-ADJ[/i]).
• ETH or RTH charts both work. The OR, breaks and ATR10 are the same on either.


[b]█ DATA AND METHOD[/b]

• NQ, ES and RTY front-month 1-minute data, Jan 2018 – Oct 2026 (ES to Aug 2026). About 6,400 opening ranges.
• OR = the high and low of 09:30–09:59. A break = a trade above the high or below the low after 10:00, by 16:14.
• ATR10 = the mean true range of the 10 prior trading days (18:00 → 17:00), so it is known before the open. ATR5 the same over 5 days.
• On the chart:
  – ATR10 and ATR5 come from daily bars, prior days only;
  – the sisters are read at the chart's timeframe, as they stood at each bar, with no lookahead;
  – nothing uses future data.


[b]█ LIMITATIONS[/b]

[i]These are historical frequencies, not guarantees.[/i]
• The odds were measured on NQ, ES and RTY. Other symbols get the pooled tables without a test of their own.
• Nothing here is financial advice.


[b]█ WHY THE NAME[/b]

[b]Zabimaru[/b], the snake-tailed baboon, is Renji Abarai's zanpakutō. Its blade splits into segments that reach out and snap back. The opening range does the same: it sets the morning's segment, then price reaches out from it in OR-sized steps. Its Bankai, [i]Hihiō Zabimaru[/i], the giant bone snake, names the session-opens section that frames the day around the range.
