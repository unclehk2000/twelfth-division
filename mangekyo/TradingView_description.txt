Mangekyō: Daily FVGs, Volume Imbalances & Suspension Blocks, Graded, with Tested Odds


════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DESCRIPTION
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

[b]Mangekyō[/b] (万華鏡, "Kaleidoscope") is the Sharingan's next stage. Sharingan reads the quarter's look back; Mangekyō reads the daily candles inside it: the higher-timeframe fair value gaps, volume imbalances and suspension blocks ICT grades from the daily chart.
[i]It maps the daily gaps and says how often gaps like them did what. It does not call the next move.[/i]


[b]█ WHAT IT DRAWS[/b]

• [b]Daily gaps[/b] from the last 20, 40 or 60 trading days, or ([b]Gaps from[/b]) only this week's and / or last week's, as ICT trades inside last week's FVG:
  – [b]FVG[/b]: BISI / SIBI, three candles whose wicks leave a gap;
  – [b]Suspension block[/b]: a candle with a volume imbalance on both sides;
  – [b]Volume imbalance[/b]: two bodies that do not meet.
• [b]The most local ones:[/b] the nearest live gaps above and below price (2 each by default), and any gap price is inside. Overlapping gaps on the same side merge: FVG (live, then inverted) before suspension block before imbalance.
• [b]Anchored as ICT draws them:[/b] an FVG or imbalance from its first candle, a suspension block from the suspended candle. Each is named by its displacement candle (▼ SIBI Thu 22 Oct).
• [b]Inversion FVGs:[/b] an FVG closed beyond is kept, in orange, from the other side, until a daily close beyond its other edge.
• [b]Daily wick CEs:[/b] the premium and discount wick CEs of the last daily swing candle, and the midpoint between them. Yesterday counts while it is a pending swing (a lower low or higher high that today has not traded past), as ICT grades Friday's candle over the weekend. A wick CE on a line of the graded gap is named in that line's label.
• [b]Grading:[/b] CE, quadrants or octants. The gap price is inside is graded as ICT grades it on the 1-minute chart: a solid line at every eighth across the chart, CE in green, fraction and price at the right end ([i]0.625 (29,577.00)[/i]); 0 = the near edge, 1 = the far edge.
• [b]PO3:[/b] when price trades inside a gap, the 2–3 daily candles that made it are drawn right of price, with the gap shaded and graded and a line at the price now.
• [b]Forming FVG:[/b] today's candle as candle 3, dashed, until the day is complete.


[b]█ DAILY DATA[/b]

• [b]Daily candles from:[/b] the chart symbol, the front-month contract (e.g. NQZ2026), the continuous 1!, or the back-adjusted 1!.
  – The unadjusted 1! jumps by the carry at each roll: 0.44–0.69 ATR in 2022–26, more than a typical daily gap is wide (0.28 ATR). Gaps from before the last roll sit at the old contract's prices.
  – A contract's own chart includes its deferred months, which traded thinly (NQ: about 0.1% of the front month's volume). Over the 60 days before each contract became front, its chart showed 5–93% more gaps than the front month did; these can be hidden.
  – The back-adjusted 1! has neither problem.
• [b]Body close:[/b] as the chart (default, so every zone lines up with its candles), the settlement (SET on), the last trade (SET off), or the wider of the two.
  – Wicks are the same either way, so FVGs don't change; imbalances and suspension blocks do.
  – 2018–26: last trade and settlement differ by more than 0.1 ATR on 12–18% of days, and the candle's colour flips on 3–5%. Only 44–50% of volume imbalances exist under both settings.
  – "Widest of the two" reads both and keeps the wider, as ICT toggles SET to find the widest imbalance; a zone read with the other close gets a dashed border.


[b]█ THE CARD[/b]

• [b]One row per gap:[/b] range, width and distance in ATR, status, and the tested odds:
  – [b]BY FRI · 20D:[/b] the chance its near edge trades by Friday and within 20 trading days;
  – [b]HOLDS · 10D:[/b] the chance the first touch day does not trade through the far edge, then that it is not closed beyond within 10 days;
  – [b]VOL · MS:[/b] the middle candle's volume against the 20 days before, and BOS if the move broke the last daily swing.
• [b]WEEK:[/b] the nearest gap above not reached before this week is the premium draw, the nearest below the discount draw, each with its by-Friday odds.
• [b]TODAY:[/b] inside a gap, where today's body sits against CE, and the odds of the next 10 days from there.


[b]█ WHAT THE TESTS FOUND[/b]

NQ, ES, RTY daily 2010–26 and YM 2017–26. Each gap was matched with 5 three-candle windows that left no gap (same symbol and direction, within two years, the same middle-candle move and candle-3 close).

• [b]Not a draw.[/b] Price returned to a gap within 20 days 84% of the time; to the same candles without a gap 86%.
• [b]A small first-touch edge.[/b] The far edge (candle 1's high or low) held the first touch day 49% of the time vs 45%. It holds in 2010–24 and 2025–26 and on NQ, ES and RTY, not on YM.
• [b]Nothing after that.[/b] Not closed beyond within 10 days of the touch: 29% vs 28%.
• [b]Volume:[/b] high-volume gaps held more only because they were wider. Against the same candles their edge was +0.3 points; at equal width, −2 points.
• [b]Structure:[/b] a break of structure added about 4 points at equal width (z 1.6), with none on NQ or in 2025–26.
• [b]The week:[/b] the nearest gap above was reached by the week 66–68% of the time, below 59–61%: the same as any level as far away. When reached, the week's extreme stopped inside it 27–30% of the time, as inside a same-size band.
• [b]ICT's body rule:[/b] when the touch day closed back outside, 44% survived the next 10 days; near half 34%; past CE 16%. Same at any level that deep (43 / 31 / 21%).
• [b]Inversion FVGs:[/b] no edge (touch day 43.5% vs 44.4%), so they are drawn, not scored.

So the reach odds come from distance and days left, and the hold odds from the gap's width.
• [b]Walk-forward 2014–26, 5 days:[/b]
  – above 6→5%, 21→22%, 41→36%, 60→57%, 81→79%, 95→94%;
  – below 6→10%, 21→22%, 40→41%, 60→61%, 80→82%, 95→95%.
• [b]FVG touch day, locked test 2025–26:[/b] 11→14%, 37→41%, 59→57%, 79→82%.


[b]█ ALERTS[/b]

Create one alert with "Any alert() function call". It covers price entering a gap, CE traded, a daily close beyond, and (optional) a new gap.


[b]█ NOTES[/b]

• [b]Charts:[/b] daily or intraday.
• [b]Repainting:[/b] daily data comes from completed days only, so it does not repaint. Today's touches and the forming FVG come from the chart's bars; a gap formed today joins the list at the next session's open.
• [b]Companions:[/b] quarterly look backs live in Sharingan, the week's own range in Getsuga Tenshō, opening gaps in OpenGaps and RTH gaps in Benihime.
