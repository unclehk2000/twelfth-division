[b]Sharingan[/b] reads the last three months to frame the next three, as ICT teaches quarterly shifts and IPDA data ranges (2017 Mentorship, Month 05):
• [b]The look back:[/b] the 60, 40 and 20 trading days left of an anchor, their highs as buy-side liquidity (BSL) and their lows as sell-side (SSL), run right while they stand and drawn bold to the candle that swept them once taken.
• [b]The cast forward:[/b] +20, +40 and +60 trading days right of the anchor, the window where ICT expects the next shift.
• [b]Daily market structure:[/b] the last two shifts (MSS) and the swing a daily close must clear to flip it back.
• [b]Quarterly Theory:[/b] the quarters (Q1–Q4) and the true year and month opens.
• [b]A card[/b] with the order flow, the structure, every level's tested odds of trading within 20 days and by +60, the window and the quarter.
• The numbers come from NQ, ES, YM and RTY daily data, 2010–26.
[i]A map of where the liquidity rests and when ICT expects the next shift. It does not call direction.[/i]


[b]█ THE ANCHOR[/b]

• [b]Last closed month[/b] (ICT's default): the first trading day of the most recent closed month. On 6 Jan 2017 that is 1 Dec 2016.
• [b]Last structure shift[/b] (calibrated): the daily high or low the last market-structure shift came from. A shift is a daily close beyond the last opposite swing; the anchor is the extreme between that swing and the shift ([i]ANCHOR 06 Oct · the high before the 14 Oct MSS[/i]).
• [b]Both:[/b] the card follows the month anchor, and the shift anchor and its marks are drawn too, fainter.


[b]█ WHAT IT DRAWS ON PRICE[/b]

• [b]The look back:[/b] each window's high (BSL) and low (SSL).
  – [b]Standing:[/b] a solid line run right to +60, named with its price ([i]SSL 60/40d 27,492.00[/i]).
  – [b]Swept:[/b] a bold line that stops at the candle that took it, ✕ there, named with the date at its origin ([i]BSL 60d · swept 02 Oct[/i]). The name sits above a high or below a low, on the side no candle reached, so it never covers price.
  – Identical levels merge into one ([i]60/40d[/i]). Brackets above price mark the three windows.
• [b]Market structure (daily):[/b]
  – the last two shifts, [b]MSS ▲ / ▼[/b]: dashed from the swing a daily close broke to the candle that broke it, named at the swing;
  – the [b]flip level[/b]: the last swing high while bearish, the last swing low while bullish, dotted and run right.
• [b]Quarterly Theory:[/b] the [b]true year open[/b] (the open of the first Monday of April, the start of the year's Q2) and the [b]true month open[/b] (the open of the second Monday), dashed and run right as references.
• [b]The cast forward:[/b] +20 / +40 / +60 marks, the +20 → +60 window shaded, and brackets above price. Future dates skip weekends and CME equity holidays.
• [b]Quarterly dividers:[/b] Jan / Apr / Jul / Oct, or ICT's Dec / Mar / Jun / Sep, each named ([i]Q4 2026[/i]), plus the next one ahead.


[b]█ CARD[/b]

• [b]Header:[/b] the quarter, the anchor and the day count ([i]Q4 2026 · anchor 01 Sep 2026 · day 37 of 60[/i]).
• [b]ORDER FLOW:[/b] the net change over the 60, 40 and 20 days left of the anchor. ICT: trading higher, frame off the low; lower, frame off the high.
• [b]STRUCTURE:[/b] bullish or bearish, the last MSS and how many days ago, and the flip level ([i]flip > 31,616.50[/i]).
• [b]LIQUIDITY:[/b] BSL above the price row, SSL below, as they sit on the chart.
  – Standing: the price, the distance from the last close in daily ATR(14), and the tested chance it trades within 20 trading days and by +60 (the window's end).
  – Swept: ✕ and the date, in bold.
• [b]WINDOW:[/b] the +20 / +40 / +60 dates, ✓ once passed.
• [b]QUARTER:[/b] the quarter and the next one, and whether price is above or below the true year and month opens.
• [b]Mobile layout:[/b] order flow, structure, the nearest standing BSL and SSL, and the window.


[b]█ ALERTS[/b]

Create one alert with "Any alert() function call". It covers:
• a look-back level taken (BSL or SSL swept);
• +20 / +40 / +60 reached;
• a new structure shift (off by default).


[b]█ WHAT THE TESTS SHOWED (NQ, ES, YM and RTY daily, 2010–26)[/b]

[b]What held: the level odds.[/b] The chance a look-back high or low trades comes from its distance in ATR(14) and the days left.
• Walk-forward 2014–26, each year fit on the years before, predicted → actual:
  – within 20 days: 3→4%, 19→20%, 39→39%, 59→58%, 79→77%, 93→95%;
  – by +60: 3→5%, 19→22%, 39→41%, 60→60%, 80→77%, 94→92%.
• Single years ran 0.7–1.3× the average; a trending year takes fewer levels on one side.
• For scale, within 20 days: a high 1 ATR away 72%, 2 ATR 49%, 3 ATR 29%; a low 62%, 39%, 25%.

[b]What the timing claims came to.[/b] Shown in the tooltips, not as signals:
• [b]Turning points[/b] came 5.3–6.6 times a year, the rate a random walk with the same volatility gives (5.8–6.5). There is no 3–4-month rhythm beyond chance.
• [b]The +20 / +40 / +60 marks[/b] held 28–31% of a 60-day window's highs and lows within ±2 days, against 25–28% by chance.
• [b]The window's ends:[/b] a window's high and low pile up at its two ends by chance (the arcsine law); days 0–2 and 58–60 hold about a third. That is why the +60 mark so often "nails" the extreme.
• [b]Structure shifts[/b] came 8.5–10.3 a year, median 20–21.5 days apart (random walk 20.7–21.6). The next one broke within 20–60 days of the last one's extreme 54–61% of the time (random walk 52–57%). MSS and the flip level frame the liquidity; they do not time the next shift.
• [b]Order flow:[/b] after a 60-day up move the 20-day low was taken first 23–30% of the time (random walk 27–32%); after a down move the 20-day high first 51–57% (44–48%). Buy-side goes first more often either way: the index drift.
• [b]Quarter starts[/b] saw turning points on 2.4–4.3% of days against a 2.1–2.6% base. That does not survive correcting for the number of tests.
• [b]Quarterly Theory, the month:[/b] in months that closed up, the low came in Q2 (the second week) 13–17% of the time, against 17–18% by chance. Q1's range broke after the true month open and the month closed on the other side 24–39% of the time, against 36–38%.
• [b]Quarterly Theory, the year:[/b] 16 years is too few to test. The true opens are drawn as references, not scored.

[b]Cast forward:[/b] the +20 / +40 / +60 marks and their colour; the shaded window.

[b]█ HOW TO USE[/b]

• Works on daily and intraday charts; it needs three months or more of daily history.
• Built for CME index futures: NQ, ES, YM and RTY (micros use the same daily levels). On a continuous chart, the back-adjusted 1! keeps the look-back levels from jumping at the rolls.
• The card sits bottom left by default, clear of the cast-forward window right of price. Move it in View if it covers your price.
• On a phone, use the Mobile layout.
• Daily fair value gaps are not drawn here: they live in Mangekyō, its next stage.


[b]█ DATA AND METHOD[/b]

• NQ, ES, YM and RTY daily candles, 2010–26.
• [b]Level odds:[/b] p = logistic(a + b · logit(2Φ(−d / (κ√h)))), where d is the distance in ATR(14) and h the trading days left; highs and lows fit separately. Scored walk-forward, each year fit only on the years before it.
• [b]Timing claims:[/b] each one against a random walk with the same daily volatility, on the same days.
• [b]Structure:[/b] a swing high (low) is the highest (lowest) daily bar of the bars three either side; a shift is a daily close beyond the last opposite swing, after structure ran the other way.
• [b]Quarterly Theory:[/b] the month's quarters are its weeks from the first full week; the true month open is the open of the second Monday, the true year open that of the first Monday of April.
• [b]On the chart:[/b] everything is read from completed daily bars (today's high and low only mark a level taken today), so nothing repaints.


[b]█ LIMITATIONS[/b]

[i]These are historical frequencies, not guarantees.[/i]
• The odds were measured on NQ, ES, YM and RTY. Other symbols get the same model without a test of their own.
• The timing marks (+20 / +40 / +60, quarter starts, MSS spacing) did not beat chance; they are drawn because ICT teaches them, and labelled as such.
• Nothing here is financial advice.


[b]█ WHY THE NAME[/b]

[b]Sharingan[/b] (写輪眼, the [i]Copy Wheel Eye[/i]) is the Uchiha clan's eye: it reads an opponent's movement so closely that it can copy a technique and see the next strike before it lands. Fully awakened, it shows three tomoe.
Here the three tomoe are the three look backs, 60, 40 and 20 trading days: what price has already done. The cast forward is the copy, the same 20 / 40 / 60 days projected ahead of the anchor, where the next shift is expected. The eye reads; it does not decide. Neither does this indicator.
Its next stage, [b]Mangekyō[/b], turns the same gaze onto the daily candles inside the quarter.
